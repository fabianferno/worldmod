and diagnostics that live inside capture, plus the shared entry, 404, offline
and the dock. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: someone with a phone strapped to their head, outdoors or in a
workshop, in daylight, who is not a crypto user and came for the money. The
job: see what this take pays, record fifteen seconds, be told plainly whether
it was accepted and why, and reach the money without recording again.

meters come back from the server, the balance comes from the escrow. Nothing on
these screens is illustrative.

present as lost; scoring survives a reload; the device-key / World-App identity
difference is recoverability and must never flatten into "wallet connected".

butter fields, ink cards, 30px lozenges, figures with a small unit rider,
full-pill controls, a black dock with a floating disc). No roll was dealt.

mint panel, with the withdraw pill directly beneath it.
